### Monopoly Junior: The Quick Rules Guide

The main goal of Monopoly Junior is to be the player with the most money when the first player goes bankrupt. It's a simplified, faster version of classic Monopoly for younger players.

---

#### **Setup**

1. **Board:** The board has 24 spaces (instead of 40).
2. **Money:** Each player starts with **$31** in specific denominations:
   - Five $1 bills
   - Four $2 bills
   - Three $3 bills
   - One $4 bill
   - One $5 bill
3. **Tokens:** Each player chooses a token and places it on **GO**.
4. **Properties:** The Chance cards are placed face-down on their spot on the board. The property cards are sorted by color and placed nearby.
5. **Who Goes First:** The youngest player goes first. Play proceeds clockwise.

---

#### **Gameplay**

On your turn, roll the die and move your token clockwise that many spaces. Then, follow the instructions for the space you land on:

1. **Property (Colored Space with Amusement)**
   - If it's **OWNED** by another player, pay them the rent shown on the space.
   - If it's **UNOWNED**, you must buy it. Pay the bank the amount shown on the space and take the corresponding property card.
   - If you own it already, nothing happens.
2. **GO**
   - Collect **$2** from the bank when you pass or land on it.
3. **Chance (Question Mark Space)**
   - Draw the top Chance card and follow its instructions (e.g., "Advance to the Water Slide", "Get out of Jail Free", "Your birthday! Collect $2 from each player"). Return the card to the bottom of the pile.
4. **Just Visiting / Free Parking**
   - Nothing happens. It's a free space.
5. **Go To Jail**
   - Move your token directly to the **JAIL** space. Do not collect $2 for passing GO. On your next turn, you must roll the die to get out (you do not need to roll a double). Any turn spent in Jail is lost.

---

#### **Key Differences from Classic Monopoly**

- **No Houses or Hotels:** You just own the properties. Rent is a fixed amount printed on the board space.
- **Set Rent:** If you own **ALL the properties of a color**, the rent is **DOUBLE** the printed amount.
- **Simplified Money:** Uses smaller, easy-to-count bills.
- **No Auctions:** If you land on an unowned property, you must buy it. If you can't, the turn ends and the property remains unowned.
- **Shorter Game:** The game ends as soon as any player runs out of money and cannot pay what they owe.

---

#### **How to Win**

The game ends immediately when one player **goes bankrupt** (can't pay what they owe). All other players count their cash. The player with the **most total money wins!**

**Tip:** A great strategy is to try to complete color sets to charge double rent, as this will make other players run out of money much faster!

Have fun playing
